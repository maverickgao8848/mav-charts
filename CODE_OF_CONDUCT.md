# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MAV Charts a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the integrity of the software.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is inappropriate or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
